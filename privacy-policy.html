Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

owndo is a personal to-do manager for Android, iOS, Linux, and macOS. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data collection

owndo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data by default. You can use the app fully offline without creating an account. Tasks, projects, reminders, and related information you enter in the app are stored locally on your device.

Third-party services

owndo does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Dropbox integration

Dropbox sync is optional. If you choose to connect owndo to Dropbox for backup or sync, your task and project data is sent directly to your own Dropbox account using Dropbox's official API. owndo does not have access to your Dropbox password and only stores the access token on your device. Your Dropbox data is governed by Dropbox's Privacy Policy.

Data storage

All app data is stored locally on your device. If Dropbox sync is enabled, a copy of your synced task and project data is also stored in your Dropbox account. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data from that device, but does not automatically delete synced data from Dropbox.

Children's privacy

owndo does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
